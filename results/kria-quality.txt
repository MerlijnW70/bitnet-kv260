  gibberish: none in 26 board answers. Every board answer is well-formed text in the language of its prompt.
  repetition: the board's only duplicated sentences in the battery that are not markdown are q22's. The other two
    board answers with a duplicate record are q19 (the fences ```python and ``` twice each, exactly as in the RTX
    4080's answer to the same prompt) and q20 (```python and the code line 'for i in range(5):' twice each, the second
    time in the truncated example). On q23, the 256-token degeneration test, the board does not trip the harness's
    repetition criterion and the RTX 4080 does: board 196 words, 12 sentences, 12 distinct, 0 duplicated,
    fell_into_repetition false; the RTX 4080 fell_into_repetition true (it repeats '- Sum = (A XOR B) AND (A AND B)'
    and '- Carry-out = (A AND B) XOR (A XOR B)', both false formulas); bitnet.cpp false, with one sentence twice.
  wrong language: none. Both Dutch prompts got Dutch answers (q21 with an English intrusion 'very' that the RTX 4080
    makes too, q26 answers about 你好 in English as asked in English).
  drift growing with the token count: not seen. The divergence positions do not fall with generation length - the
    longest identical generations are q08 (90 ids) and q16 (77 ids off a 124-id prompt), and the longest generation in
    the battery, q23 at 256 tokens, held the RTX 4080's ids for 102 tokens and then stayed coherent for the remaining
    154 while the RTX 4080's own continuation was the one that repeated. q24 run twice gave byte-identical ids both
    times, so the board is deterministic.
  crash or corruption: none. The empty-ish prompt q25 ('?', 16 prompt ids) generated 24 ids and stopped on EOS,
    byte-identical to the RTX 4080's. The unusual code points of q26 round-tripped prompt -> tokenizer -> runtime ->
    decoder. The code answers came back with intact ``` fences, 4-space indentation and an unmangled [::-1].

CONCLUSION
  No board answer is gibberish, none is in the wrong language, none drifts further from the reference as it gets
    longer, and the only repetition in a board answer that is not a markdown fence is q22_nl_uitleg, where the RTX
    4080 running the same weights fails the same prompt just as completely and bitnet.cpp on the same board
    degenerates further. On that evidence the pipeline is sound.
  Where the board differs from the RTX 4080 running the same model: 11 of 26 generations are byte-identical, ids and
    text, including 90 ids on q08 and 77 ids on q16 off a 124-id prompt, and q24's determinism pair. The other 15
    share a prefix and then take one different token: 12 of those forks are at generated position 4 or later (54, 42,
    41, 41, 24, 11, 11, 11, 10, 9, 4 and 102) and each is a synonym- or punctuation-level substitution - 'particularly
    effective' for 'particularly useful', 'circuits bright' for "circuits' heart", 'of the string' for 'of the input
    string', 'number of iterations' for 'number of times' - after which the board's continuation stays fluent. Three
    fork at position 0 (q12, q22, q26). That is the shape of an integer and shift-quantised datapath meeting bf16 at a
    near-tied logit, and it matches the measured teacher-forced agreement (98.7% of board tokens are the RTX 4080's
    top-1, 100% inside its top-5, every disagreement on an HF logit gap of 0 to 0.5) and the board's --stage-check,
    which found every integer stage exact at 8 positions through all 30 layers. The judges called the board's answer
    worse than the RTX 4080's on 0 of 26 prompts, equivalent or identical on 16, and different but fine on 10; on q11
    (12:05, where bitnet.cpp says 12:20), q12 (mouse, horse, elephant, where the RTX 4080 puts the elephant second),
    q19 (the reversed-string comment, character-exact where the RTX 4080 loses two spaces) and q23 (no repetition,
    where the RTX 4080 repeats) the board's answer is the better one.
  What belongs to the 2B model and not to the hardware: the wrong syllogism (q10, the same error on all three ways,
    and 34 consecutive ids shared with bitnet.cpp), the circular CRC-32 explanation (q03, emitted identically by the
    RTX 4080), the haiku that is not 5-7-5 (q17, the RTX 4080's is not either), the 'four lines' that is three
    sentences (q18, likewise), the false claim that a for loop takes a condition (q20), the wrong carry-out formula
    (q23, identical on the RTX 4080), the circled-plus read as addition and the micro sign read as the Greek letter
    (q26, both wrong the same way on the RTX 4080), and the Dutch: q21 answers correctly and then produces
    'populestat', 'een very bekend stad' and 'kantine' filler that the RTX 4080 produces in the same idiom, and q22
    fails on all three ways. BitNet b1.58 2B4T is trained mostly on English; the Dutch quality measured here is a fact
    about the model, demonstrated on the GPU, not about the fabric.
